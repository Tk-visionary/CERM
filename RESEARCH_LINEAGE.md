# Research lineage

CERM keeps the installable library and its research record separate.

The public repository contains promoted library code, package tests,
documentation, examples, and release engineering. Experimental studies,
negative results, preregistrations, benchmark protocols, and research-lineage
metadata are maintained in a separate research workspace.

That research record will be published separately after its own provenance,
privacy, and release audit. Until then, no public URL is provided here.

A research mechanism is not considered part of the public CERM contract merely
because it was explored during development. Promotion into this repository
requires an explicit implementation and validation decision.
